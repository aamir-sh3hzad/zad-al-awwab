## Frequently Asked Questions

### Prayer times look incorrect for my location
Go to **Settings → Prayer Calculation** and choose the calculation method used in your region (e.g., ISNA, Muslim World League, Umm al-Qura, Karachi). Also make sure your location is correct under **Settings → Location**.

### I'm not receiving prayer notifications
1. Check that notifications are allowed: iOS **Settings → Zad Al-Awwab → Notifications → Allow Notifications**.
2. For reliable alerts, grant **"Always"** location access so prayer times update while the app is in the background.
3. Make sure Adhan notifications are enabled under **Settings → Notifications** in the app.

### The Qibla compass points the wrong way
Move your device in a figure-8 motion to recalibrate the compass, away from magnets or metal surfaces.

### How do I change the app language?
Open **Settings → Language** and pick from 10 supported languages: English, Arabic, Urdu, Turkish, Indonesian, Malay, French, German, Spanish, and Italian.

### Does the app work offline?
Yes. Prayer times, Qibla, Quran, Duas, Azkar, Tasbih, and the 99 Names of Allah all work offline once your location is set.

### How do I report a bug or request a feature?
Email us at **aamir@fullstryx.com** with a description of the issue, your device model, and iOS version. Screenshots help!
