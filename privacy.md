# Privacy Policy — Zad Al-Awwab

**Last updated: September 2026**

Zad Al-Awwab ("the App"), developed by FullStryx Solutions ("we", "us"), respects your privacy. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and your rights.

## 1. Data We Access

### Location Data
- **Purpose:** calculating accurate prayer times, Qibla direction, and finding nearby mosques.
- **Scope:** the App requests "When In Use" and optionally "Always" location access so prayer-time notifications stay accurate.
- **Storage:** your coordinates are stored **on your device only**. We do not transmit your location to our servers.

### Device Motion
- **Purpose:** powering the Qibla compass. Motion data never leaves your device.

### Notifications
- **Purpose:** prayer-time and Adhan alerts. Scheduled locally on your device.

## 2. Third-Party Services

The App links the following SDKs. In the current release, account features are disabled and no account data is collected.

| Service | Purpose | Data processed |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| Adhan (open source) | Prayer time calculation | None — runs on-device |
| Apple MapKit / CoreLocation | Mosque maps, directions | Location used on-device for map queries |
| Apple CoreMotion | Qibla compass | None — on-device only |
| Apple UserNotifications | Prayer alerts | None — scheduled locally |
| Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Analytics, Messaging) | Account sync & analytics infrastructure | **Not active in this release** — no account or analytics data is collected while login is hidden |
| RevenueCat | Subscription entitlements | **Not active in this release** — no purchases are processed while AI features are disabled |

## 3. Data We Do NOT Collect

- No name, email, or account information (login is disabled in this build)
- No analytics or advertising identifiers
- No photos, microphone, camera, or speech data
- No location data is transmitted off your device
- No tracking across other apps or websites

## 4. On-Device Data

Bookmarks, favorites, reading progress, Tasbih counts, and settings are stored locally on your device and are deleted when you delete the App.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

If we enable account, analytics, or subscription features in a future release, we will update this policy before release. Continued use of the App after an update means acceptance of the updated policy.
