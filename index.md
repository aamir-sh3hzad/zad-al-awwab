# Zad Al-Awwab

**Prayer Times, Quran & Qibla — your free Islamic companion.**

Zad Al-Awwab helps you stay connected to your daily worship:

- 🕌 Accurate prayer times with 12+ calculation methods and Adhan notifications
- 🧭 Real-time Qibla compass with distance to the Kaaba
- 📖 Complete Holy Quran with translations, bookmarks, and reading progress
- 🤲 Authentic Masnoon Duas, morning & evening Azkar, and Wazaif
- 📿 Digital Tasbih counter and the 99 Names of Allah (Asma ul Husna)
- 🗺️ Nearby Masjid finder with directions and distance
- 🌙 Hijri calendar, dark mode, and 10 languages

All core Islamic content is completely free — no sign-up required.
